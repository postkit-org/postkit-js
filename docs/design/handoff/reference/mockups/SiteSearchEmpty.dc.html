Get started

Introduction. Rich posts that render right everywhere, from one plain-text file.

Postkit gives your writing 35 ready-made components, from callouts to code to galleries, and renders them on your site, in your newsletter and in your editor. You keep your design, your framework and your content.

What you get

Write once

Your writers use MDX or plain Markdown. The same 35 components work in both, and typos fail the build, not the reader.

Look like your site

Use the headless views with your own CSS, or the Chakra views with your theme. Readers never see a template.

Publish everywhere

The same file renders on your site, in your newsletter, in feeds and in Prismark, without a second copy to keep in sync.

Never boxed in

Restyle a part, swap a view, or eject the source into your repo. Your posts keep working at every step.
